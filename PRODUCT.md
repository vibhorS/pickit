# Problem

People constantly discover good ideas throughout the week.

Instagram reels.

Friends.

Articles.

TikToks.

Reddit.

YouTube.

But when the moment to decide actually arrives...

"What should we watch tonight?"

"What should we eat?"

"What should we cook?"

...they either forget everything or spend 45 minutes deciding.  
  
# Vision

Build the world's best collaborative decision platform.

Not a recommendation engine.

Not a watchlist.

A place where people continuously collect options together,

then effortlessly make decisions when the moment comes.
